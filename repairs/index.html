Not on the list?

We would rather say “test first” than sell the wrong repair.

Some transmission codes point to mechanical gear-selection problems, vehicle wiring, auxiliary pumps, clutch wear or a broader internal gearbox fault. Those are not converted into a postal TCM repair just because the car has a DSG gearbox.

Use the Repair Finder, or send the exact scan wording, vehicle details and controller label.

DQ200 electronics

U0101 / 01315 no communication is handled separately.

The established DQ200 TCM electronics service remains on TCMRepair.co.uk. That keeps the electronic no-communication repair separate from the £399.99 DQ200 hydraulic/mechatronic pressure service.
