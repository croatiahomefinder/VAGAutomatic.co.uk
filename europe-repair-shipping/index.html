UK & Europe

Send your unit for repair and return.

European customers can send their own used TCM or mechatronic to our UK workshop. The important part is declaring the shipment accurately as a temporary repair-and-return movement, not a sale.

Courier description

Suggested plain-language description
Used automotive transmission control unit / mechatronic temporarily sent to the United Kingdom for repair and return to owner. No sale. Ownership unchanged.

Use your courier's repair-and-return, temporary export or outward-processing option where applicable. Keep the original export documents because they may be needed when the repaired unit comes back.

Do not do this

  • Do not mark it as a gift.
  • Do not deliberately understate the value.
  • Do not describe a used unit as a new retail purchase.
  • Do not discard the export / tracking paperwork before the unit returns.

What to put in the box

  • Your name, phone and email
  • Vehicle make, model and registration / VIN if available
  • TCM / mechatronic part number
  • Exact fault code and scanner wording
  • Selected service: Standard or Same-Day Priority
  • Your return shipping instructions / prepaid return label where arranged

Customs requirements vary by country and courier. This page is practical shipment guidance, not customs or tax advice. Follow your courier's formal declaration process.
