UK workshop · Europe mail-in

Send your DSG unit for repair and return.

European customers can send their own used TCM or mechatronic to our Halesowen workshop. The customer arranges and pays the courier in both directions. For cross-border shipments, describe the movement accurately as a temporary repair-and-return shipment, not a sale.

Carriage

Customer pays shipping both ways.

You choose and pay the courier to our workshop and you also arrange/pay return carriage. You can include a prepaid tracked return label in the parcel or email the return label before the repaired unit is ready to leave.

Repair prices do not include courier charges. This applies to UK and European customers.
Customs description

Describe what the parcel actually is.

Used automotive transmission control unit / mechatronic temporarily sent to the United Kingdom for repair and return to owner. No sale. Ownership unchanged.

Where your courier offers a repair-and-return, temporary export or outward-processing option, use the option appropriate to your shipment and country. Keep the original export and tracking paperwork because it may be needed when the repaired unit returns.

Do not do this

A repair shipment is not a gift or a retail purchase.

  • Do not mark the parcel as a gift
  • Do not deliberately understate the value
  • Do not describe a used customer-owned unit as a new retail sale
  • Do not discard the export / tracking paperwork before the repaired unit returns
Packing checklist

Make the parcel easy to identify and safe to return.

Strong boxUse enough cushioning that the unit cannot move inside the parcel.
Your detailsName, phone, email and country.
Vehicle detailsMake, model, year, registration/VIN if useful.
Part numberTCM/mechatronic label or a printed photo of the label.
Fault informationExact DTC and full scanner wording, not just “gearbox fault”.
Selected serviceStandard or Same-Day Priority.
Return carriagePrepaid tracked return label in the box or email it before dispatch.
Export paperworkKeep a copy of the courier/customs paperwork for the return journey.
Important

Customs rules depend on the country and courier.

This page provides practical repair-shipment wording, not legal, tax or customs advice. The courier's formal declaration and your local customs requirements take priority. If your courier asks for a value, procedure code or additional evidence, provide accurate information and follow their instructions.

Our printable support form helps identify the parcel as a customer-owned unit sent for repair and return. It does not replace the courier's customs declaration.
