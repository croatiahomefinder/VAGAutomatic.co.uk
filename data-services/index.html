Data services

TCM cloning and data recovery.

Sometimes the electronics are not being repaired for use as-is. The job is to preserve the original transmission-controller data and move it to a compatible replacement, or recover that data from a damaged controller before it is lost.

TCM Cloning

Original TCM data transferred to a compatible donor.

£99

Supply the original controller and a compatible donor. We read the original data, transfer the required vehicle-specific information and verify the donor where the hardware is suitable.

Handled through our dedicated TCMRepair electronics bench to keep one canonical service page for this work.

View TCM Cloning service →
Damaged / burnt TCM

Recover the data when the controller itself may be beyond normal repair.

£149

For burnt, physically damaged or non-communicating transmission controllers where the important question is whether the original vehicle data can still be extracted for a replacement unit.

Recovery depends on the condition of the original electronics, so damaged units are assessed before any promise is made.
