# VAG Automatic V4.2 Monster

## New public pages
- `/known-faults/` searchable DSG fault library with green / amber / red routing
- `/dq500-identification/` Bosch vs Continental identification guide
- `/about/` technical review, workshop approach and trust page

## Expanded fault coverage
- DQ200 selector-position, pump-related, input-speed and clutch-control fault families added as diagnosis-led routes
- DQ250 added to the fault library as diagnosis-led only, with sender/wiring screen-outs kept separate from TCU faults
- DQ381 companion/adaptation codes added without turning them into fixed-price promises
- DQ500 Bosch companion/procedure codes added
- DQ500 Continental expanded with P0607, speed-signal, P/N signal and gear-monitoring fault families

## Search and UX
- Repair Finder expanded for the above aliases and symptom phrases
- Known Fault Library can be filtered by gearbox family and searched by code, VAG number or symptom
- Navigation now links to fault library, DQ500 identification and About
- Homepage links into the deeper fault content without becoming a giant wall of text

## Trust / conversion
- Technical-review strip on core repair and category pages
- Cloning page clarifies why the original controller and a compatible donor matter
- Fixed-price vs test-first vs vehicle-diagnosis routes are visually separated

## What remains private
- Exact pressure-offset test thresholds
- EEPROM/calibration methods
- Internal sensor replacement procedure
- Supplier-specific repair systems
- Stock quantities and donor strategy
