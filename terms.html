Service Terms

Fixed-price supported repairs

Published fixed prices apply to the stated supported fault on a unit that is suitable for repair. Previous repair attempts, severe physical damage, corrosion or unrelated faults may mean the fixed repair cannot be completed. We will tell you before charging for additional work.

No repair, no fee

If we cannot complete the supported repair, there is no repair labour charge. Courier costs and other third-party costs remain the customer's responsibility.

Turnaround

Standard turnaround is 2 to 3 working days from receipt. Same-Day Priority applies where the unit arrives before 12:00 noon on a working day and the supported fault is confirmed and repairable. Courier transit time is separate.

Warranty

Completed repairs carry a 2-year warranty against recurrence of the fault we repaired. It does not cover unrelated new faults, gearbox mechanical damage, hydraulic failures outside the repair scope, water ingress, incorrect refitting or external vehicle faults.

Consumer rights

Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under UK consumer law.
