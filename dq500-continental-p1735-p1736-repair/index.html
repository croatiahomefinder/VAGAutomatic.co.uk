Symptoms

Does this sound like your car?

  • Gearbox emergency mode
  • Loss of one clutch / gear set
  • P1735 or P1736 stored
  • Intermittent recovery after restart
Diagnostic wording

Codes and descriptions we recognise.

P1735Clutch 1 pressure / position-sensing fault on supported Continental DQ500 hardware.
P1736Clutch 2 pressure / position-sensing fault on supported Continental DQ500 hardware.
P0607 / speed-signal / P-N signal faultsThese may be repairable but are test-first cases, not automatically included in this fixed-price P1735/P1736 service.

ODIS, VCDS, Autel, Launch, Topdon and other scanners can phrase the same manufacturer fault differently. We match the DTC, VAG symptom number where available, controller hardware and fault meaning rather than one exact sentence.

Repair quality

The part inside matters.

Correct controller generation and compatible repair components are confirmed before work. We do not treat every Continental DQ500 fault as the same repair.

We deliberately avoid building a repair around anonymous lowest-cost components simply because they are cheaper. The supported repair is backed by our 2-year repair warranty.

What to send

Continental DQ500 TCM / mechatronic control unit after label confirmation.

Do not send unrelated gearbox parts unless we have asked for them. If you are unsure which controller you have, send a clear photo of the label first.
